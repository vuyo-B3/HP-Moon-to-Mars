How we work together

A short weekly call keeps everything moving, and the campaign timeline is the shared reference. We circulate a written summary after each call, and that summary is what both sides work from.

Weekly call, every Tuesday

We run through bottlenecks, what is live that week, and anything at risk against the timeline. We raise HP owned dates the week before they land, so nothing is a surprise. The current version of the campaign timeline is always the reference, and topics for the weeks ahead are added there.
