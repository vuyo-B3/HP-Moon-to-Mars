The programme in three phases

Phase 1 carries the campaign into the holiday buying window. Phases 2 and 3 build on the same roster once the first content is proving out.

Live now

Phase 1, YouTube first

Eight to twelve multichannel creators across product design and manufacturing, and architecture, engineering and construction. Each one gets a workstation, a workflow session with HP, and produces a core YouTube film plus cross platform posts. Target go live, first week of October.

Next

Phase 2, LinkedIn

Six LinkedIn first creators reaching the procurement and IT decision makers who sign off hardware. Names to be confirmed once Phase 1 is in market.

Next

Phase 3, amplification

Tech publishers, meme pages and newsletter placements extending the reach of the content that performs. Sourcing begins once Phase 1 results are in.

Phase 1 is organic. No paid media and no new lead capture in this phase. Creator content carries an organic find out more link to the HP store, aimed at prosumers and small businesses, the same audience the AEC pilot landing page pulled. Any offer attached to that link sits with the store team, and any amplification goes through Edelman.
