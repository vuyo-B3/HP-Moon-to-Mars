        What we did to build this list

        HP approved seven creators out of the first list and rejected the rest without telling us why. Rather than wait for that feedback we worked backwards from the profiles they said yes to, found the pattern, and sourced against it.

        The two audiences we are sourcing for

        PD&MProduct Design and Manufacturing. Mechanical services, factory design, machine creation, prototyping a physical product.
        AECArchitecture, Engineering and Construction. Designing and managing large structures, public works, building information modelling.

        The pattern in what HP approved

        Every approved creator is a practitioner educator who demonstrates their own work rather than reviewing other people's. They teach a named professional workflow, they sit mostly between 100K and 1M subscribers, and their comment sections are calm and technical.

        Dami Lee was the one rejection with a reason attached, cut over negative comments that were not handled well. That tells us HP is auditing community health rather than reach alone, so every candidate here was checked on that specifically.

        How each candidate was checked

        • Competitor conflict, no Apple, Dell or Lenovo work in the last six months
        • Sponsor history, whether existing partners sit aligned or competing
        • Comment section health, tone and how the creator handles criticism
        • Posting consistency and whether performance holds on branded content
        • Channel spread, and whether they meet the two platform minimum
        • Software mapped to HP's own ISV list, Revit, SOLIDWORKS, Fusion, Rhino, Civil 3D

        How the 21 break down

        Twelve are core fit, meaning they match the approved pattern closely and we would put them in front of HP with confidence. Four sit adjacent, credible and clean but a step outside the core workflow audience, offered as reach and balance options. Five are consumer craft creators, sourced and vetted but not recommended for this phase, included so HP can see the full search rather than a filtered slice.

        Two things worth raising with HP. Four of the strongest candidates run only one or two channels with nothing Stories capable, because the deepest practitioner educators tend to live on one platform and teach. And CG Geek has run an HP sponsored video before, which makes him the warmest lead on the list, but he has a recent ASUS ProArt relationship, so that call belongs to HP rather than us.
